Introduction

Ari's Tea

My love for boba and exploring different tea shops inspired me to create Ari’s Tea. I wanted to create my own brand that combines quality tea, fun flavors, and a modern take on Asian tea culture. Inspired by the tea shops I love, Ari’s Tea aims to offer delicious drinks and a welcoming experience that customers can enjoy anytime.

Target audience

Teens and young adults

For those who enjoy boba, specialty teas, and trying new drinks. Drawn to trendy shops, unique flavors, and brands that offer quality products with a modern, aesthetic experience.
