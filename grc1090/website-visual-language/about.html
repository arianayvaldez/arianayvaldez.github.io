Ari’s Tea began with my love for boba and discovering new tea shops. I wanted to create a place that brings together the drinks and flavors I enjoy most.

Inspired by Asian tea culture, Ari’s Tea pairs quality tea bases with fun, modern flavors. Each drink is made to feel special, whether it is a classic milk tea or something more unexpected.

Our menu includes creamy milk teas, premium matcha, refreshing fruit teas, and dessert-inspired signature drinks. There is something for every mood and every kind of tea lover.

Ari’s Tea is made for hanging out with friends, trying something new, or finding your everyday favorite. We hope every visit feels welcoming, fun, and worth coming back for.
